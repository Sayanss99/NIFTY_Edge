How the Market Tone is built
The verdict blends 7 reads into one bullish/bearish call. Each read votes up, down or neutral, and counts by a fixed weight — the bigger the weight, the more it moves the verdict (and the brighter its tile).
Reading the tiles: green = that read points up today, red = points down, grey = neutral. Brighter = heavier weight. The verdict adds up every read by its weight; the badge shows the net lean and “% of weight agrees” is how much of the total weighting points that way. Numbers refresh live with the option chain & institutional flows.
