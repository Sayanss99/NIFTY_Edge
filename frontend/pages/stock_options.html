What the Safety Rating means
The green bar scores how solid a pick looks right now, out of 5. Each filled mark = one quality check the trade clears — more green means higher conviction. (DeMark isn't part of this score; it only decides Picks vs Radar.)
1
Momentum (RSI pullback)
The stock is in a healthy pullback (it's in our RSI watchlist) — not free-falling and not over-stretched.
2
IV sweet spot (IVP)
Option premiums are richly priced — good for a seller — but not at a dangerous extreme.
3
Safe strike distance (Delta)
The strike sits a sensible distance out-of-the-money: far enough to be lower-risk, close enough to still pay.
4
Worthwhile return (Premium yield)
The premium pays enough (annualised) to be worth the risk of selling it.
5
Trend alignment (Forward vs spot)
The futures price agrees with the trade's direction — the market isn't leaning the other way.
6
Setup confirmation (Anchored VWAP)
Since the setup began, price is holding on the trade's side of the volume-weighted average — the reversal is actually working, not just signalled.
6/6 clears every check (highest conviction). Whatever a pick misses is listed as a “filter exception” under Details on its card — so a 4/6 still trades, you just see what it skipped.
