CE Strike Distance (OTM pts)
How far OTM your sold CE strikes sat at entry, bucketed every 200 pts. Lighter bar = all trades in bucket; solid overlay = winners. Read it as: do calls placed further OTM win more often?
PE Strike Distance (OTM pts)
Same idea for sold PE strikes. Use this to spot the distance band where your puts actually survive — your historical "safe cushion" on the downside.
Strike Distance vs P&L
Each mark is one closed option leg. X-axis = OTM distance at entry, Y-axis = realised P&L. Circles are CE, squares are PE; green = winner. Clusters in the top-right mean "far OTM worked"; losses near the left mean strikes were too close to spot.
